## 現代数学教育についてのノート

2025年8月から "Mathematics and Computational Thinking" Education と勝手に呼んでいる分野の研究を細々と行っています。この活動に関連して得られた雑学レベルの知見を日本語でまとめた文書をこちらに置くことにします。


## Notes on Contemporary Mathematics Education

Since August 2025, I have been quietly conducting research in a field I’ve taken to calling "Mathematics and Computational Thinking" Education. I have compiled some of the miscellaneous insights at the level of trivia gained through this work into a document in Japanese, which you can find here.

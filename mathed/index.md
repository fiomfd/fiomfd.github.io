   - GitHub: ATCM2025, [https://github.com/fiomfd/ATCM2025)](https://github.com/fiomfd/ATCM2025).
   - Google Colab: ATCM2025, [https://colab.research.google.com/github/fiomfd/ATCM2025/](https://colab.research.google.com/github/fiomfd/ATCM2025/).
 - 査読付き英文原著論文：*Implementing computational thinking in primary and secondary mathematics*, [The Mathematician Educator](https://ame.org.sg/tme/), accepted for publication.
